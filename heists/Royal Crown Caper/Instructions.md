🔑 Step 0: Role Selection
	•	🕵️ Hacker → re-themed as Infiltrator (handles gates, locks, secret passages).
	•	💪 Muscle (Female) → fends off guards, climbs walls, carries the crown chest.
	•	🎭 Con Artist → poses as nobles, entertains guards, distracts royals.
	•	🚗 Driver → themed as Carriage Master/Pilot (final escape via carriage, horses, or helicopter).

⸻

💰 Payout System
	•	Base Loot = 3.5M × players.
	•	Bonus treasures (gems, chalices) collected in group stages.
	•	Suspicion Meter = Alert Level.
	•	Alert ≥ 3 → castle gates lock, heist ends in failure.

⸻

🏰 Stages

⸻

🟢 Stage 1: Festival Entry (Con Artist Test)

Narration:
“The royal courtyard is filled with jugglers, merchants, and nobles. Guards check invitations at the gate.”

Action:
	•	🎭 Con Artist rolls (d100 ≥ 70).
	•	If no Con Artist → all stealth rolls (≥ 60).

Failure: Alert +1.

⸻

🔵 Stage 2: Scaling the Walls (Muscle Test)

Narration:
“The quickest way inside is over the castle walls, but the climb is steep.”

Action:
	•	💪 Muscle rolls (d100 ≥ 75).
	•	If no Muscle → everyone rolls (≥ 55).

Failure: Lose 10% loot (drop gear while climbing).

⸻

🟡 Stage 3: Courtyard Patrol (Group Stealth Test)

Narration:
“The courtyard bustles with guards patrolling in formation.”

Action (All Players):
	•	Each rolls stealth (d100 ≥ 55).
	•	Any failure = Alert +1.

⸻

🔴 Stage 4: The Guard Dogs (Full Group Puzzle)

Narration:
“A pack of trained hounds blocks the hallway to the throne room.”

Action:
	•	Emoji reaction challenge: 🐕🍖➡️🚪.
	•	All players must react in the right sequence.
	•	Any fail = Alert +1.

⸻

🟣 Stage 5: Throne Room Bluff (Con Artist Test)

Narration:
“A chamberlain intercepts you in the throne room, asking your purpose.”

Action:
	•	🎭 Con Artist rolls persuasion (d100 ≥ 70).
	•	If no Con Artist → pay 15% loot bribe.

Failure: Alert +1.

⸻

🟤 Stage 6: The Crown Vault (Infiltrator + Muscle Combo)

Narration:
“The crown is locked inside a jeweled vault with a heavy iron gate.”

Action:
	•	🕵️ Infiltrator rolls (d100 ≥ 75) to unlock the gate.
	•	💪 Muscle rolls (≥ 70) to push open the iron door.
	•	If both succeed → silent entry.
	•	If one fails → door opens but Alert +1.
	•	If no Infiltrator/Muscle → fallback riddle: “The more you take, the more you leave behind. What am I?” → Footsteps.

⸻

⚫ Stage 7: Jewel Grab (Full Team Test)

Narration:
“Beside the crown are other treasures: chalices, gems, golden scepters.”

Action (All Players):
	•	Each rolls (d100 ≥ 60).
	•	Success = +1 royal jewel (worth 1M).
	•	Fail = Alert +1.

Optional Greedy Grab:
	•	Roll ≥ 80. Success = +2 jewels. Fail = Alert +2.

⸻

🟣 Stage 8: Great Hall Escape (Group Challenge)

Narration:
“As you move through the great hall, guards rush in. You must dodge arrows and spears to reach the exit.”

Action:
	•	Group emoji sequence: ⬆️⬅️⬆️➡️⬇️⬆️.
	•	All players must succeed.
	•	Any fail = Alert +1 + lose 25% loot.

⸻

⚪ Stage 9: Carriage Chase (Driver Test)

Narration:
“You burst out of the castle gates with the crown. The guards give chase.”

Choices:
	•	🐎 Horse Carriage: Driver rolls (d100 ≥ 70). Fail = crash, -50% loot.
	•	🏍️ Royal Motorbikes (disguised as knights): Driver rolls (≥ 75). Success = +10% loot. Fail = wipeout, -25% loot.
	•	🚁 Helicopter Snatch: Costs 20% loot. Auto success.

⸻

💰 Rewards
	•	Base = 3.5M × players.
	•	Bonus jewels from Stage 7.
	•	+50% if Alert = 0.
	•	-25% if Alert > 0.
	•	Alert ≥ 3 = locked gates, fail.
	•	Legendary 5% drop → “Crown Thief” title.
