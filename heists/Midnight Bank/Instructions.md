🔑 Step 0: Role Selection
	•	🕵️ Hacker → disables security cameras, cracks safes.
	•	💪 Muscle → handles guards, brute force entry.
	•	🎭 Con Artist → bluffs staff, talks way past obstacles.
	•	🚗 Driver → escape with armored truck or tunnel van.

⸻

🟢 Stage 1: Rooftop Entry (Con Artist Test)

Narration:
“The bank is closed, but two night guards smoke on the rooftop. If you’re spotted, it’s over.”

Action:
	•	🎭 Con Artist rolls persuasion (d100 ≥ 65) to bluff as maintenance staff.
	•	If no Con Artist → all players roll stealth (≥ 55) to sneak past.

Failure: Suspicion +1 (guards radio suspicious activity).

⸻

🔵 Stage 2: Camera Network (Hacker Test)

Narration:
“Inside, the walls are lined with security cameras. One wrong move and alarms trip.”

Action:
	•	🕵️ Hacker rolls (d100 ≥ 65) to disable the feed.
	•	If no Hacker → Group typing race: first to correctly type “DISABLE” within 5 seconds wins.

Failure: Suspicion +1.

⸻

🟡 Stage 3: Guard Encounter (Muscle Test)

Narration:
“A patrolling guard heads straight for the vault corridor.”

Action:
	•	💪 Muscle rolls (d100 ≥ 70) to knock him out silently.
	•	If no Muscle → group vote:
	1.	🎭 Con Artist bribes (lose 10% loot).
	2.	Everyone rolls stealth (≥ 50).

Failure: Alarm triggered → suspicion +2.

⸻

🔴 Stage 4: The Vault Door (Hacker + Muscle Combo)

Narration:
“The vault has a digital keypad and a massive steel bar across the door. Both must be broken.”

Action:
	•	🕵️ Hacker rolls (≥ 70) to bypass keypad.
	•	💪 Muscle rolls (≥ 65) to lift the steel bar.
	•	If both succeed → clean entry.
	•	If one fails → entry delayed, suspicion +1.
	•	If no Hacker/Muscle → fallback = riddle puzzle (e.g., “What has keys but can’t open locks?” → Piano).

⸻

🟣 Stage 5: Cash Grab (Full Team Test)

Narration:
“Stacks of cash are piled inside the vault. You only have minutes before backup arrives!”

Action (All Players):
	•	Everyone rolls (d100 ≥ 50).
	•	Each success adds +1 stack of cash (worth 500k).
	•	Each fail = suspicion +1.

Optional:
	•	Players may attempt Greedy Grab (target 70+, worth 1M if success, suspicion +2 if fail).

⸻

🟤 Stage 6: Escape Tunnel (Group Puzzle)

Narration:
“The alarms blare! You sprint through an underground tunnel filled with old pipes and dead ends.”

Action:
	•	Group maze puzzle using emoji directions (⬆️⬇️⬅️➡️).
	•	All players must enter the correct sequence.
	•	Any mistake → suspicion +1 and loot -25%.

⸻

⚫ Stage 7: Final Getaway (Driver Test)

Narration:
“The tunnel opens into the city streets. Police sirens close in. Your escape depends on the driver.”

Choices:
	•	🚛 Armored Truck: Driver rolls (d100 ≥ 65). Fail = crash, lose 50% loot.
	•	🏍️ Motorcycles: Driver rolls (≥ 70). Success = +10% loot bonus. Fail = wipeout, lose 25% loot.
	•	🚁 Helicopter Extraction: Costs 15% loot, auto success.

⸻

💰 Rewards
	•	Base Loot = 1,000,000 × # of players.
	•	Extra stacks from Stage 5.
	•	+50% if suspicion = 0.
	•	-25% if suspicion > 0.
	•	Suspicion ≥ 3 = heist fails, all loot lost.
	•	5% Legendary Drop → “Vault Breaker” title.
