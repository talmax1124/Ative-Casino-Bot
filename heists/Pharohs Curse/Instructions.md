🔑 Step 0: Role Selection
	•	🕵️ Hacker → here re-themed as Archaeologist (deciphers hieroglyphs, disables ancient mechanisms).
	•	💪 Muscle → breaks through stone doors, fends off guardians.
	•	🎭 Con Artist → charms locals, interprets symbols, talks the group through cursed rooms.
	•	🚗 Driver → themed as Scout/Driver (guides escape caravan, pilots chopper out of the desert).

⸻

💰 Payout System
	•	Base Loot = 1.5M × players (higher stakes).
	•	Extra treasure from group stages (gold artifacts, jewels).
	•	Suspicion here = “Curse Meter.”
	•	If Curse Meter ≥ 3, everyone is trapped forever (total failure).

⸻

🌑 Stages

⸻

🟢 Stage 1: Desert Entry (Con Artist Test)

Narration:
“Local guards patrol the dig site. Can you convince them you belong?”

Action:
	•	🎭 Con Artist rolls charm (d100 ≥ 65).
	•	If no Con Artist → everyone rolls stealth (≥ 55).

Failure: Curse Meter +1 (the gods notice your intrusion).

⸻

🔵 Stage 2: Collapsing Stairway (Muscle Test)

Narration:
“As you descend, a stone staircase collapses beneath you!”

Action:
	•	💪 Muscle rolls strength (d100 ≥ 70) to catch falling stones.
	•	If no Muscle → everyone rolls reflex (≥ 55).

Failure: Loot -10% (crushed artifacts lost).

⸻

🟡 Stage 3: Hieroglyph Puzzle (Hacker/Archaeologist Test)

Narration:
“A sealed stone door is covered in hieroglyphs. Only the right sequence will open it.”

Action:
	•	🕵️ Hacker/Archaeologist rolls (d100 ≥ 65).
	•	If no Hacker → Group puzzle: unscramble “PHARAOH” within 15 seconds.

Failure: Curse Meter +1.

⸻

🔴 Stage 4: The Hall of Spears (Full Group Test)

Narration:
“The hallway ahead is lined with hidden spear traps. Only perfect teamwork will get you through.”

Action:
	•	Each player types the correct emoji sequence (➡️⬆️⬇️⬅️).
	•	ALL players must succeed.
	•	Failures = Curse Meter +1 each.

⸻

🟣 Stage 5: Guardian Statue (Muscle Test)

Narration:
“A massive stone guardian awakens, blocking your way to the treasure chamber.”

Action:
	•	💪 Muscle rolls (d100 ≥ 75).
	•	If fail or no Muscle → everyone rolls group attack (≥ 60).

Failure: Curse Meter +1.

⸻

🟤 Stage 6: The Treasure Grab (Full Team Test)

Narration:
“The tomb bursts open, filled with gold, jewels, and artifacts. But the chamber shakes as a curse awakens!”

Action:
	•	Each player rolls (d100 ≥ 55) for treasure grab.
	•	Success = +1 artifact (worth 500k).
	•	Fail = Curse Meter +1.

Optional Greedy Grab:
	•	Roll ≥ 70. If success = +2 artifacts. If fail = Curse Meter +2.

⸻

⚫ Stage 7: Sandstorm Escape (Driver Test / Group Choice)

Narration:
“The desert rages with a sandstorm. You must escape before the tomb collapses completely!”

Choices:
	•	🚛 Caravan Escape: Driver rolls (d100 ≥ 70). Fail = -50% loot.
	•	🏍️ Motorcycle Run: Driver rolls (≥ 75). Success = +10% loot bonus. Fail = -25% loot.
	•	🚁 Helicopter Rescue: Costs 20% loot. Auto success.

⸻

💰 Rewards Recap
	•	Base = 1.5M × players.
	•	Extra artifacts (500k each) from Stage 6.
	•	+50% if Curse Meter = 0.
	•	-25% if Curse Meter > 0.
	•	Curse Meter ≥ 3 = total fail, team lost to the tomb.
	•	Legendary 5% drop → “Cursebreaker” badge.
