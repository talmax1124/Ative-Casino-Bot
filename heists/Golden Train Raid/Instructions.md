🔑 Step 0: Role Selection
	•	🕵️ Hacker → disables train security and smart locks.
	•	💪 Muscle → handles guards, breaks doors, lifts heavy crates.
	•	🎭 Con Artist → distracts passengers, bluffs as inspector.
	•	🚗 Driver → coordinates the final getaway (motorcycles, truck, or helicopter).

⸻

🟢 Stage 1: Boarding the Train (Con Artist Test)

Narration:
“The high-speed train barrels through the night. Security is scanning tickets at the platform. Can you get your crew onboard?”

Action:
	•	🎭 Con Artist rolls charm (d100 ≥ 60) to fake tickets.
	•	If no Con Artist → everyone rolls stealth (≥ 55) to sneak on.

Failure: Suspicion +1 (guards recognize stowaways).

⸻

🔵 Stage 2: Locked Carriage (Hacker Test)

Narration:
“The gold is in the rear cars, behind a locked smart-door system.”

Action:
	•	🕵️ Hacker rolls (d100 ≥ 65) to override the lock.
	•	If no Hacker → Group typing challenge: first player to type the correct code word (e.g., “GOLDEN”) opens the door.
	•	Failure = suspicion +1.

⸻

🟡 Stage 3: Guard Patrol (Muscle Test)

Narration:
“A pair of guards walk the aisle, scanning each passenger. If they spot you, it’s over.”

Action:
	•	💪 Muscle rolls (d100 ≥ 70) to take them down silently.
	•	If no Muscle → group vote:
	1.	Con Artist distraction (lose 10% loot).
	2.	Everyone stealth roll (≥ 50).

Failure: Alarm triggered → suspicion +2.

⸻

🔴 Stage 4: Shifting Cargo (Muscle + Hacker Combo)

Narration:
“The gold car is locked with both a heavy steel crate and a digital seal. It will take teamwork to crack it.”

Action:
	•	💪 Muscle rolls (d100 ≥ 65) to lift and move the crate.
	•	🕵️ Hacker rolls (d100 ≥ 70) to bypass the digital seal.
	•	If both succeed → full success.
	•	If one fails → vault opens but suspicion +1.
	•	If no Muscle or Hacker → fallback puzzle: unscramble “TRAIN” in under 20 seconds.

⸻

🟣 Stage 5: Gold Grab (Full Team Test)

Narration:
“The crates are open, filled with bars of gold. But the train is shaking, and security shutters are closing!”

Action:
	•	Every player rolls a grab check (d100 ≥ 50).
	•	Each success = +1 additional gold bar (500k coins each).
	•	Each fail = suspicion +1.

Optional Risk:
	•	A player may declare Double Grab (target = 70+). Success = +2 bars (1M coins), failure = suspicion +2.

⸻

🟤 Stage 6: Rooftop Chase (Group Action)

Narration:
“The alarms finally go off! You sprint onto the roof of the speeding train. Spotlights flash overhead. Security helicopters are incoming.”

Action (Group Puzzle):
	•	Everyone must type an emoji sequence (⬆️⬇️⬅️➡️) in order.
	•	All players must succeed.
	•	Any mistake = suspicion +1.

⸻

⚫ Stage 7: The Getaway (Driver Test)

Narration:
“The train nears the city. You need a perfect exit before reinforcements arrive.”

Choices:
	•	🚛 Truck Pickup: Driver rolls (d100 ≥ 65). Success = clean escape. Fail = crash, lose 50% loot.
	•	🏍️ Motorcycles: Driver rolls (≥ 70). Success = quick exit, +10% loot bonus. Fail = wipeout, lose 25% loot.
	•	🚁 Helicopter Lift: Pay 15% loot. Auto success.

⸻

💰 Rewards
	•	Base Loot = 1,000,000 × # of players.
	•	Extra gold bars from Stage 5.
	•	+50% if suspicion = 0.
	•	-25% if suspicion > 0.
	•	Suspicion ≥ 3 = heist fails, team caught.
	•	Legendary 5% drop → “Golden Bandit” title.
