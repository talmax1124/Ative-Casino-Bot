# Real-World Scenarios - Balanced Economy System

## 🎯 Design Philosophy

**The Three-Pronged Approach:**
1. **Gentle Tax/Decay** - Billions drain over MONTHS, not days
2. **Aggressive Multiplier Scaling** - Wealthier players get worse game odds (NO MAX BETS)
3. **House Edge Scaling** - Additional payout reduction based on wealth

**Result:** Millions are comfortable, hundreds of millions sustainable with effort, billions drain slowly but inevitably.

---

- Top user: ~$16.1B
- 5 users in billions range
- Many users in millions range

**Goal:** Let them keep playing, but slowly bring billions down to tens/hundreds of millions over 2-3 months.

## 📊 Scenario 1: Comfortable Million-Dollar User

**Starting Wealth**: $2,000,000 (2M)

### Daily Income Potential
- **Work** (20x daily): 1,500 × 20 = 30,000 base
  - With diminishing returns: ~25,000 actual
- **Daily reward**: 2,500 × 1 = 2,500
- **Crime** (10x daily): 1,000 × 10 = 10,000 base
  - With diminishing returns: ~8,000 actual
- **Gambling** (moderate):
  - Slots: Bet $10K, win rate ~30%, avg return $8K per game
  - Blackjack: Bet $20K, multipliers at 95%, avg return $18K per game
  - Roulette: Small bets, near-full odds

**Total Daily Income Potential**: ~$50,000-$80,000

### Daily Costs

**Decay**:
- Bracket: 1M-10M → multiplier = 1.0
- Decay rate: 0.0001 × 1.0 = 0.01% daily
- Decay amount: $2M × 0.0001 = **$200/day**

**Tax**:
- Bracket: 1M-10M → 0.05% daily
- Tax amount: $2M × 0.0005 = **$1,000/day**

**Total Daily Costs**: ~$1,200/day

### Net Result
**Income**: $50,000-$80,000
**Costs**: $1,200
**Net**: **+$49K to +$79K/day profit**

✅ **VERY SUSTAINABLE!** Can easily maintain and grow wealth. Millions are comfortable!

## 📈 Summary: The Three-Tier System

### Tier 1: Millions ($1M-$50M)
- **Tax/Decay**: 0.01%-0.2% daily (minimal)
- **Multiplier Scale**: 70%-100% (excellent odds)
- **House Edge**: 1%-2% (fair)
- **Status**: ✅ SUSTAINABLE - Comfortable and fun!

### Tier 2: Hundreds of Millions ($50M-$500M)
- **Tax/Decay**: 0.2%-0.8% daily (noticeable)
- **Multiplier Scale**: 15%-55% (reduced odds)
- **House Edge**: 3.5%-8% (tough)
- **Status**: ⚠️ MAINTAINABLE - Requires skill and luck

### Tier 3: Billions ($1B+)
- **Tax/Decay**: 0.8%-1.15% daily (heavy)
- **Multiplier Scale**: 4%-8% (severe reduction)
- **House Edge**: 12%-15% (extreme)
- **Status**: ❌ UNSUSTAINABLE - Drains over months

---

## 🎯 What Makes This System Work?

1. **NO MAX BETS** - Players feel unrestricted
2. **Gentle Tax/Decay** - Doesn't feel punishing day-to-day
3. **Aggressive Multiplier Scaling** - The real control mechanism
4. **Transparent** - Players see exactly how their wealth affects odds
5. **Gradual** - Billions drain over MONTHS, allowing continued play
6. **Fun Factor** - Millions remain exciting and achievable
