# Real-World Scenarios - Bulletproof Economy V2

Based on current economy state:
- Top user: ~$16.1B
- 5 users in billions range
- Many users in millions range

## 🎯 Design Goals

1. **Millions ($1M-$10M)**: Comfortable, maintainable, achievable
2. **Tens of Millions ($10M-$100M)**: Attainable but requires effort to maintain
3. **Hundreds of Millions ($100M-$1B)**: Very difficult to maintain, constant pressure
4. **Billions ($1B-$10B)**: Unsustainable, massive daily losses
5. **Tens of Billions ($10B+)**: Mathematically impossible to maintain
6. **Trillions**: Impossible to reach

---

## 📊 Scenario 1: Comfortable Million-Dollar User

**Starting Wealth**: $2,000,000 (2M)

### Daily Income Potential
- **Work** (20x daily): 1,500 × 20 = 30,000 base
  - With diminishing returns: ~25,000 actual
- **Daily reward**: 2,500 × 1 = 2,500
- **Crime** (10x daily): 1,000 × 10 = 10,000 base
  - With diminishing returns: ~8,000 actual
- **Gambling** (moderate): Variable, ~20,000 average

**Total Daily Income Potential**: ~55,000

### Daily Costs

**Decay** (wealth-dependent):
- Bracket: 1M-10M → multiplier = 1.0
- Decay rate: 0.0005 × 1.0 = 0.05% daily
- Decay amount: $2M × 0.0005 = **$1,000/day**

**Tax** (bracket system):
- Bracket: 1M-10M → 1% daily
- Tax amount: $2M × 0.01 = **$20,000/day**

**Transaction Fees** (if transferring):
- Minimal impact for small transfers

**Total Daily Costs**: ~$21,000/day

### Net Result
**Income**: $55,000
**Costs**: $21,000
**Net**: **+$34,000/day profit**

✅ **Sustainable!** Can easily maintain and grow wealth in millions range.
